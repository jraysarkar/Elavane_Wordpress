# Legal pages (drafts)

Elvane's Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions, written for an Indian online
clothing store (IT Act 2000 and rules, DPDP Act 2023, Consumer Protection
(E-Commerce) Rules 2020). They replace text copied from Razorpay's own policies.

**Drafts: have them reviewed by a lawyer before going live, and fill in every
`[Confirm: …]` (grievance officer, support email/phone, delivery days, return
window and rules, effective date).**

On a site: `wp post update <page id> privacy-policy.html` (Privacy Policy page,
Settings → Privacy) and `terms-and-conditions.html` (the page set in
WooCommerce → Settings → Advanced → Terms and conditions).
