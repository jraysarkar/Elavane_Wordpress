This Privacy Policy explains how ELVANE CLOTHING ("Elvane", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you visit elvaneclothing.com or buy from us. It is published under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020. By using the website you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

ELVANE CLOTHING, a firm registered under GST with GSTIN 19AANFE0751H1ZC, R.S.392, L.R.342, Nazrul Sarani, North Sukanta Nagar, P.O. Rabindra Sarani S.O., P.S. Bhaktinagar, Jalpaiguri, West Bengal 734006, India. We decide how your personal data is used (we are the "Data Fiduciary").

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your mobile number (used to log in with a one-time code), and your name, email address and password if you choose to add them.
  • Order details: billing and delivery address, phone number, email, the products you buy, order notes and, if you give it, your GSTIN for a business invoice.
  • Payment details: online payments are handled by our payment partner Razorpay. We do not see or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank login; we only receive the payment status and a reference number.
  • Usage details: your cart, wishlist, pages viewed, device and browser type, IP address and approximate location, collected through cookies and similar technologies (see section 6).
  • Messages: anything you send us by email, phone or social media.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data such as health, biometric or financial account details.

3. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and log you in with a one-time code.
  • To process, ship and deliver your orders, send order and delivery updates, and handle returns, exchanges and refunds.
  • To issue GST invoices and keep the records the law requires.
  • To answer your questions and complaints.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, and to keep the website secure.
  • To improve the website and our products.
  • To send offers and news, only if you have agreed; you can unsubscribe at any time.

We use your data with your consent, which you give by using the website and placing orders, or where the law allows or requires it (for example, tax records).

4. Who we share it with

We share only what each partner needs to do its job, and we do not sell your personal data.

  • Payments: Razorpay, to process online payments and refunds.
  • Login: Google Firebase, to send and verify your one-time login code.
  • Delivery: our shipping aggregator iCarry and its courier partners, who receive your name, phone number and delivery address.
  • Email: our email delivery provider, to send order and account emails.
  • Hosting and website tools: our hosting provider and the analytics and social media tools described in section 6.
  • Authorities: government or law-enforcement bodies when the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Some of these providers may store data outside India, on servers with safeguards in line with Indian law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account while it is active. Order and invoice records are kept for as long as tax and accounting laws require (for GST records, at least six years). Other data is deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed for the purposes above.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. We use them to keep you logged in, remember your cart and wishlist, keep the website secure and understand how it is used. Our Instagram feed and any analytics or advertising tools we use may set their own cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but the cart and login may then stop working.

7. How we protect it

The website uses encrypted connections (HTTPS), logins are protected by one-time codes, and access to customer data is limited to people who need it. No system is completely secure, but we follow reasonable security practices as required by Indian law and will inform you and the authorities of a personal data breach as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or update it (you can also do this in My account);
  • delete it, except what we must keep by law;
  • withdraw your consent, for example to marketing messages (this does not affect what was done before);
  • nominate someone to act for you in case of death or incapacity.

Write to the Grievance Officer below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

The website is meant for people aged 18 or over. If you are under 18, please use it only with the involvement of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect children's data without a parent's or guardian's consent.
